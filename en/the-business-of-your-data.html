THE BUSINESS OF YOUR DATA

You hand over your data for free and they sell it

You open a newspaper. Before it finishes loading, your machine has asked for twenty or thirty addresses that do not belong to the newspaper: companies that buy and sell the ad slot you are about to see, and that need to know who you are in order to auction it. You do not choose them, nobody introduces them, and you could almost certainly not name one. This page names them.

How it works, in four steps

  1. You open a site. The site has advertising slots it sells to the highest bidder.
  2. The auction takes milliseconds, and to bid you need to know who the ad is for: where you are, what you looked at before, which device you are on.
  3. For that, several companies have to agree that "you" are the same person who was on another site yesterday. That is called matching identifiers, and some names say so out loud: `user-sync`, `sync`, `prebid`.
  4. None of this is charged to you. You pay for it with the data.

What this page does NOT say

  • It does not say these companies do anything illegal. What they do is published on their own sites and rests on a consent most people accept without reading. The only thing this page asserts about anyone's legality is what a regulator or a court has already ruled, and where that happened it is on their card, with the date and a link to the file. Nothing else.
  • It does not say what data of yours each one holds. GUARDIANA sees service names, never content: we know your machine asked for that address, and nothing more.
  • It does not say a penalty from years ago still describes the company today. The date is beside it so it is read with it.
  • If you are one of these companies and a figure is wrong, write to hola@guardianagroup.com and it is corrected with its date, as everywhere else on this site.

And what GUARDIANA has to do with it

GUARDIANA does not block ads and does not promise to take you out of this business. It does something smaller and harder to argue with: it shows you the names. When one of these services appears in your ledger, it comes with its company, its country and what it sells. Then you decide.
