TERMS OF USE AND REFUNDS · 10 OCTOBER 2026

Plain terms, refund with no questions asked.

1 · What it is

GUARDIANA is an open-source program (GPL-3.0-or-later) for Windows and macOS that turns your PC into the DNS guardian of your home. There is one plan, and it covers both programs, GUARDIANA and GUARDIANA ZERO: each one works in full for a 7-day trial, with no card and no account, and after that you need a subscription, monthly or yearly, cancellable whenever you like.

What happens if you do not subscribe when the trial ends: GUARDIANA does not switch off. It moves to GUARDIANA Free, for good, and keeps watching this computer with the DNS as it is: the live globe, every program’s DNS with its country, company and category, your own blocks and their undo, and the last 24 hours of history. The rest is Plus: the whole history, up to 5 computers, alerts, automatic blocks by category and lists, certificate and rights letters, reports, Home Mode, encrypted communications and GUARDIANA ZERO. If Home Mode was on, the other devices of the house keep getting answers so nobody is left without internet, but Free does not look at them or write them down. What it had already written down stays whole on your disk and you can export it as CSV, JSON or PDF whenever you want; nothing is deleted, not even years later, and none of it ever reaches us.

It includes GUARDIANA ZERO. The subscription also includes GUARDIANA ZERO, the browser for Windows 10 and 11, which has its own 7-day trial, with no card and separate from GUARDIANA’s: the same key works for both programs. If its trial ends without a subscription, the browser keeps opening pages, but without its protection (it does not cut trackers, advertising or data brokers, clean addresses, watch your marked data, or make mandates or redactions).

The code is open and will stay open. It is published whole under GPL-3.0: if you can compile it, the program is yours, free, with no time limit and without asking us. What you pay for here is the program already made — compiled, signed, tested on Windows and macOS, with the lists refreshed with each release — and a person who answers.

2 · What it does not promise

GUARDIANA sees service names. It does not see the content, or which app asked for them on a phone. It acts only on your home Wi-Fi. It is not an antivirus and not parental control. And it does not see what goes around DNS, such as a VPN or an encrypted DNS of its own. We do not guarantee “total security”, nor protection against anything in particular. The full list of limits is on the site and inside the program itself. Using it is your decision and your responsibility, under the terms of the GPL-3.0 licence.

3 · Buying and the licence

Payment is made to the payment gateway, which acts as merchant of record, issues the invoice and handles taxes. You will receive a licence key by email. The key is personal and covers up to 5 computers: yours, in one home or several. It cannot be resold. Activating with a key makes one connection to the gateway. After that, the program checks the key again: a subscription, 8 days after activation and then once per payment period (monthly or yearly); a Lifetime licence, 8 days after activation and then every 30 days. If a check cannot be made (no network, or the gateway does not answer), it tries again every hour. If the gateway says the subscription is not paid, Plus switches off (if you pay afterwards, it comes back when you type the key again). And if a subscription goes 7 days without being checked, counted from the first failed attempt, Plus switches off too: GUARDIANA moves to Free and keeps watching this computer, just as when the trial ends. The program keeps trying every hour and, as soon as a check succeeds, Plus comes back by itself, without typing the key. GUARDIANA ZERO follows the same rules with the same key: if its subscription switches off this way, the browser keeps opening pages, but without its protection, until a check succeeds. The Lifetime licence never switches off for a check it could not make (point 3 bis). Every connection is written into your ledger.

The price depends on the billing country you give when paying; the gateway may decline the payment if that country does not match your means of payment. Plus monthly: US$2.49 in Latin America (Colombia included), €4.49 in Europe, £3.99 in the United Kingdom and US$4.99 in the rest of the world. Plus yearly, 20% less: US$23.90, €43.10, £38.30 or US$47.90. Prices already include any applicable taxes.

The program's 7 trial days are not charged and ask for no card; the subscription is charged when you buy it. A subscription can be cancelled at any time and ends at the end of the period already paid for; the price of a period already paid does not change. Cancelling the renewal does not refund the current period, except as point 4 says about the 30 days. Monthly and yearly have exactly the same features. Plus is a licence to use the program: it is not a stake in the company and not a financial product.

What happens to your history when the subscription ends: everything lives on your computer and nothing reaches us. Nothing is deleted, ever, not even years later: the program stops writing, but what was written stays where it is. You can export it as CSV, JSON or PDF from the ledger page, or delete it yourself whenever you want.

3 bis. The Lifetime licence

Until 18 October 2026 it was sold as the Founder licence. Whoever bought it then is an Original Founder: the licence, its key and everything below stay exactly the same.

One payment, no renewal, limited to 1,000 licences worldwide. US$49 in the rest of the world, US$24.50 in Latin America (Colombia included), €44.10 in Europe and £39.20 in the United Kingdom, with any applicable taxes already included. When the 1,000 are sold it is withdrawn; the number is not extended. It gives everything Plus gives, with no monthly or yearly fee.

What “for life” means, said plainly: for as long as GUARDIANA exists as a product. We do not promise the program will last forever, because that is not in our hands and saying it would be a lie; we promise that while it exists, you never pay again. If we ever stopped publishing it, the copy you have installed would keep working on your machine — it is open source and depends on no server of ours — and we would say so on this site, with the date.

What it covers and what it does not: it is personal, covers up to 5 computers of yours, and cannot be resold or transferred. It includes later versions of the program, 2.0 and GUARDIANA 2 too when they ship. It does not expire for lack of a connection: the program checks the key 8 days after activation and then every 30 days (if it cannot, it tries again every hour), but it never switches Plus off for a check it could not make, even if the machine spends months turned off. The one thing that ends it is a refund: the gateway then stops treating it as valid and the program says so, with the date, on the Licence page. The 30 days of no-questions refund in point 4 apply to the Lifetime licence too.

The refund is processed by the gateway to the same payment method. You do not need to pay to try it: the 7-day trial asks for no card.
