How it is measured, and house rules

House rules

  • Only measured facts, with the date and the version.
  • Always against something. With browsers, five at a time, and at least one of them sold as private (Brave, DuckDuckGo or similar); the first x-ray, on 14 September, compared two, and says so.
  • What the maker does well, and what you can switch off yourself, is said too.
  • The full data to download, and the method to repeat it.
  • Never “spy” and never “malicious”: GUARDIANA sees names, not what travels inside.

How these are made

The machine's DNS points at GUARDIANA, which writes down every name it is asked to resolve, with the time and the device. Each page or application is opened alone, in its own time window, so the names can be attributed. The complete list of names goes out as a CSV next to each x-ray, and the tools are in the program's repository. If you are the maker of something measured here and a figure is wrong, write to hola@guardianagroup.com and it is corrected with the date.

Agent receipt

The same idea, applied to an AI that works for you: what services the machine looks up while it does a task. The protocol was published before the first figure, on purpose: the method is fixed first and measured afterwards.
