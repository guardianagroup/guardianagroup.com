GUARDIANA ZERO 1.0.1 · 7 DAYS FREE

The browser that shows you what it is saving you from.

GUARDIANA ZERO is a browser of our own for Windows. While you browse, you see at once which companies each page calls behind your back and which of them are cut off, with their country and the reason. The first 7 days are free, with no account; after that it comes with the GUARDIANA subscription. No server of ours: everything happens on your computer.

Download GUARDIANA ZEROWindows 10 and 11 · 7.6 MB · 7 days free · hash and signature

What it does

  • It cuts what websites call behind your back: trackers, advertising and the companies registered as data brokers. The website you open works; what is cut is the companies from outside.
  • It shows you live. Each tab’s shield says how many companies tried and how many were cut off. “What was cut” shows every cut one by one, with the company, its country, the kind of request and the reason, and saves it as PDF or CSV.
  • It removes tracking tags from addresses (utm, gclid, fbclid…) before they leave.
  • It rejects cookie notices from the most used consent managers (OneTrust, Cookiebot, Didomi and others) by pressing «Reject all»; if the notice has no such button, it hides it without accepting anything. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Your favourites, with the star in the address bar, as quick links on the new tab. You can bring yours from Chrome, Edge or Brave in one click. They are kept only on your computer.
  • Your marked data (your email, your phone…): ZERO stops it going out to another company when it detects it, even turned into code the way ad networks do, and before a form sends it, it asks you.
  • A mandate for your AI: a tab that only goes to the websites you name, for as long as you allow, and leaves a signed receipt of what it did.
  • Redaction: prepares what you are going to ask an AI with your personal data struck out, and puts it back in place in the answer.

Opening it for the first time

Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”, because the program does not carry a publisher certificate yet. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. If double-clicking does nothing, right-click the file, choose “Properties”, tick “Unblock” at the bottom and click “OK”; then open it again. Why this happens, and how to check the file before opening it, is explained on the install page: the signature is checked the same way as GUARDIANA’s.

What it does not do

  • It does not make you anonymous, least of all to a website where you are signed in, and it does not stop every way of recognising a computer by its fingerprint.
  • It is not a VPN: the websites you visit still see your IP address.
  • It only sees what goes through the browser. What the other programs on the computer do is seen by GUARDIANA, the program.
  • The web engine is WebView2, by Microsoft, the same as Edge’s: it comes with Windows 10 and 11 and Windows keeps it up to date.

What it costs

The first 7 days are free, with no card and no account, and they count separately from GUARDIANA’s. After that, GUARDIANA ZERO comes with the GUARDIANA subscription: the same key works for both programs. If the trial ends without a subscription, the browser keeps opening pages, but without protection: it does not cut trackers, advertising or data brokers, clean addresses, watch your marked data, or make mandates or redactions; only the web engine’s basic protection remains, the same as Microsoft Edge’s. To activate the key it connects once to Dodo Payments, the payment gateway, and after that checks it once per billing period; and if you press “Check for updates”, it reads the public ledger on GitHub once. Every connection is shown, with its date, under “Subscription”. Anything that looks wrong, write it to hola@guardianagroup.com: it is corrected with a date.
