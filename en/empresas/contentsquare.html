COMPANY PROFILE

Contentsquare

Headquarters: Paris, France · Founded: 2012

What it does and how it makes money

French company that sells businesses analytics software to see how visitors use their websites and apps (clicks, scrolling, session recordings) and improve them. It owns Hotjar and Heap.

It sells businesses access to its cloud software platform (SaaS). [1]

What data it collects about you

On its clients' sites it records your IP address, user and cookie IDs, pages viewed, browser and device, mouse or touch movements, scrolling and clicks (including session replay) and your country; it may capture keypresses if the client enables it. It does this as a processor on behalf of each client. [2]
