COMPANY PROFILE

Integral Ad Science

Headquarters: New York, United States · Founded: 2009

What it does and how it makes money

Integral Ad Science (IAS) is a US ad verification and measurement company: it checks that digital ads are seen by real people in suitable environments, for advertisers and publishers. It was founded in 2009 as AdSafe Media and has been owned by the fund Novacap since December 2025.

It charges advertisers and publishers a fee per thousand ads it analyses, largely through ad-buying platforms (DSPs). [1]

What data it collects about you

Its website privacy policy says it collects visitors' IP address, device identifier, browser, operating system, log data and cookies; data its measurement technology collects when analysing ads is covered by a separate policy we could not review. [2]
