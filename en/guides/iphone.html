GUIDE · HOME MODE

iPhone and iPad

Useful when you do not want to touch the router, or the router will not let you change the DNS. It works only for the home Wi-Fi: on mobile data, the iPhone does not go through GUARDIANA.

Steps

  • In GUARDIANA's panel › “Home Mode”, note the PC's address (for example 192.168.1.39).
  • On the iPhone: Settings › Wi-Fi › tap the (i) next to your home network.
  • Scroll down to Configure DNS › Manual.
  • Delete the servers listed (the red button) and tap Add Server.
  • Type the PC's address. If you want a fallback for when the PC is off, add the router's address below it (usually 192.168.1.1); GUARDIANA will not see those queries.
  • Save (top right).
  • Open Safari and go to http://comprobar.guardiana.hogar. If it loads “This device already goes through Guardiana”, you are done. If it does not load, turn the Wi-Fi off and on and try again.

What you should know

  • iCloud Private Relay (Settings › your name › iCloud › Private Relay) makes Safari resolve names on its own: those queries do not pass through GUARDIANA. GUARDIANA does not block it; it records it as “traffic out of sight” when it detects it.
  • With a VPN active, nothing passes through GUARDIANA either.
  • This change applies only to that Wi-Fi network. On other networks the iPhone uses its normal DNS.

Undoing it

Settings › Wi-Fi › (i) › Configure DNS › Automatic. Do this before turning the guardian PC off for a long time, or the iPhone will be left without DNS at home.
