HOW IT WORKS

How GUARDIANA works

GUARDIANA is used from this website, with no account and nothing to install. There are four services: the ZERO browser opens each website for you, Comunicaciones encrypts your messages and files end to end, Órbita draws the internet’s infrastructure and NÚCLEO guides you to what you need. This is what each one does on the inside, and what it does not do.

THE ZERO BROWSER

Each website opens far from your device, and you decide what gets cut.

ZERO opens the website in an isolated browser, the “bubble”, which runs on a GUARDIANA server in Nuremberg (Germany) or in Miami (United States): whichever responds first. Only images of the page reach your device, and the website sees the server’s IP address, not yours.

1

YOU TYPE A WEBSITE

Or you search for it with Google, with Brave or, if you choose, through the Tor network.

2

ZERO GOES IN BEFORE YOU

It checks malware and fraud lists, the certificate, the downloads and the letters that imitate others.

3

THE BUBBLE OPENS IT

If there is a serious risk, it does not go in. If there is a sign of risk, it warns you and you choose.

4

YOU DECIDE

You see which company is behind each connection, which country it is from and what it is for, and you can cut it with a switch.

DECOYS

Each bubble carries marked decoy data. If any of it goes out to another company, ZERO shows it to you with the evidence: which piece of data went out, where to and when.

ON CLOSING

The bubble is destroyed when you close the tab, after 30 minutes with the tab hidden or after 12 hours, along with its cookies, its cache and its tabs. Before that, a record summarises what ZERO did during the session.

ZERO is a demonstration version: it may be slow or fail on some websites. With Google and with Brave it cuts nothing by default, except dangerous websites: you choose. With Tor, it cuts advertising and tracking from the start.

COMUNICACIONES

Messages, files and calls between two people, with no account or phone number.

Everything is encrypted in your browser before it leaves. The GUARDIANA server that carries it, the relay, passes on packets it cannot read and keeps no names or keys; if the other person is not connected, it keeps the message in a sealed envelope that only their phone can open, until it is delivered (7 days at most).

1

YOU CREATE AN INVITATION

A 9-digit code, valid for 10 minutes and for a single use.

2

THE OTHER PERSON TYPES IT IN

You give it to them by phone or through any messaging app.

3

YOU CHECK THE NUMBER

Compare the safety number with the other person through another channel: if it matches, nobody has got in the middle.

4

YOU WRITE, SEND AND CALL

Chat, voice notes, files of up to 4 GB, calls and video calls. When you finish, the conversation is deleted from your browser and its deletion is requested in the other person’s.

ENCRYPTION

Messages and files use a post-quantum hybrid key agreement (ECDH P-256 and ML-KEM-768) and a different AES-256-GCM key for each message and each file chunk. The shielded session uses the algorithms of the CNSA 2.0 suite, with one classical signature and one post-quantum signature. Calls and video calls use DTLS-SRTP, the standard encryption for browser calls; in Chrome and Edge a post-quantum layer is added when both devices have it enabled, and the call screen indicates it.

DELIVERY RECEIPT (ULTRA, COMING SOON)

Each file arrives in chunks that are checked one by one. At the end, both browsers sign a receipt with the file’s SHA-256 fingerprint, which anyone can check at Verify a receipt.

What it cannot guarantee: encryption does not protect a device or a browser that is already compromised, nor does it prevent the recipient from keeping copies or taking screenshots. The relay sees the IP addresses, the room, the times and the volume of data: this is not anonymity.

ÓRBITA

The internet’s infrastructure, on a single map.

Submarine cables, terrestrial cables, data centres and satellites, with data from public sources that is checked before publication. The sources and their licences are in the terms of use (point 6).

MY CONNECTION

What any website knows about you is read in your browser. If you ask, Órbita measures your connection from outside and the round-trip time to 33 data centres. The paths it draws are illustrative: they are not the real route of your data.

THE JOURNEY OF YOUR DATA

A PDF or video report, made in your browser, with the measured connections on the planet. Available on computers.

NÚCLEO

A guide that takes you to what you need.

NÚCLEO understands specific requests with its own engine, without generative artificial intelligence, which runs in your browser and does not send what you ask to any server. It takes you to the ZERO browser, to the planet or to Comunicaciones, and prepares PDF or video summaries of what ZERO has detected.
