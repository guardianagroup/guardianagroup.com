YOUR AI

You asked it to sort the invoices. Do you know how many services your machine looked up?

An artificial-intelligence agent works with your keys: your folders, your accounts, your network. You give it permission for a task and it does the task; what you do not know is how many places it tries to reach along the way, because nobody shows you. GUARDIANA sits between your machine and the internet and shows you, so you can hand the work to the agent without standing over it. And if you want it stopped as well, you write which services it needs and turn on Guard mode: anything not on that list is cut there and then and waits for you to decide, name by name. With one warning we give you before you switch it on: it applies to that whole device, not only to the agent, because GUARDIANA sees names and not programs.

The limit, said here and in the program: GUARDIANA checks where your agent talks, not which files it opens nor what travels inside each connection. An agent that only touches files on your disk asks for no name and does not show up. Neither does what leaves through a VPN or an app’s own encrypted DNS, which is recorded when detected.

WHAT IT SEES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

DNS. Almost everything that goes out to the internet starts by asking «where is this service?», and GUARDIANA is what answers that question on your PC (and in your home, with Home Mode). It is not a proxy and not a driver: it does not sit in the middle of the traffic and never opens what you send or receive; it only sees the name that was asked for, the time and which device asked.

HOW IT WORKS

Before your device can connect to any service, it has to ask where that service is. That question goes through GUARDIANA.

GUARDIANA answers those questions from inside your computer and writes down what it sees: the service name, the time and which device asked. Never the content. With Home Mode, phones and the TV too, without installing anything on them.

TRAP FILES

A trap that tells you who opened it, without reading a single file

GUARDIANA can leave a file on your desktop that looks like a copy of your keys. There is no key inside: there is one unique internet address that exists nowhere else in the world. If anything (an AI, a browser extension, a web page) reads that file and goes looking for that address, the question passes through GUARDIANA and is written down with the time and, on Windows, with the name of the program that asked. GUARDIANA never opened the file and does not know what is inside: it keeps looking only at names, as it does with everything else.

What this trap does not catch, said before you ask: a program that reads the file and sends it to its own server without looking up that address will not show. It is a trap for whoever follows the link, not a theft detector.

Step by step, on your system

The summary. Below, each point is explained slowly, with what you will see on screen and why.

Windows 10 and 11

Four steps

  1. Download guardiana-1.0.0-windows-x86_64.msi. It appears on this page on 5 October.
  2. Check the hash before opening it, in PowerShell:
    Get-FileHash guardiana-1.0.0-windows-x86_64.msi -Algorithm SHA256
    and compare it with the one in the public ledger. If they are not identical, delete the file.
  3. Open it. Windows shows this warning. It does not say the program is dangerous: it says Windows does not know it yet.
  4. Once installed, type guardiana verify: it repeats the check by itself, on your machine.
Linux with systemd

Four steps

  1. Download guardiana_1.0.0_amd64.deb (or the .tar.gz if you are not on Debian or Ubuntu).
  2. Check the hash and the signature:
    sha256sum guardiana_1.0.0_amd64.deb
    minisign -Vm guardiana_1.0.0_amd64.deb -P <key>
    The public key is already published.
  3. Install:
    sudo apt install ./guardiana_1.0.0_amd64.deb
    On Linux there is no unknown-publisher warning: there is a hash and a signature, which can be checked, unlike a warning.
  4. Check the install with guardiana verify.
macOS 13 or later

Four steps

  1. Download GUARDIANA.app.zip and check the hash:
    shasum -a 256 GUARDIANA.app.zip
  2. Open it. macOS stops it with this warning. Careful: the button selected by default is "Move to Trash". The one that closes the warning without deleting anything is "OK".
  3. System Settings › Privacy & Security, and at the very bottom, under "Security", press "Open Anyway". Once is enough.
  4. Check the install with guardiana verify.

Right-click › Open no longer works on current versions of macOS: we tried it on 19 September 2026 and the same warning appears, with the same two buttons. If someone tells you right-click fixes it, they are repeating something Apple removed.

WHY YOU MAY SEE A WARNING WHEN INSTALLING

Windows warns about what it does not know yet, signed or not. That is why we teach you to check the file.

In exchange for waiting for the signature you get something better than a seal: the full code, a reproducible build and the public hash of every version. You verify, or anyone you trust.

Windows shows a blue warning to installers its reputation system does not know yet. Without a publisher certificate it always appears; with one it still can, because since 2024 that reputation is earned through downloads and no certificate grants it, not even the expensive ones. We do not sign it today, and here is why: a seal costs money and does not remove the warning, so we would rather spend that on keeping the program free and checkable. We do not teach anyone to skip warnings: we teach you to verify. Once you check the file’s hash and signature, the warning no longer matters, because you no longer depend on Windows telling you whether to trust it.

It is also a technical decision: we do not intercept your traffic, we do not read content, we have no user account and no server holding your data. Less surface, less to attack.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What people ask us by email.

Does it replace my antivirus?

No. Your antivirus watches the files on one PC; GUARDIANA watches who each device on your Wi-Fi talks to. They complement each other. Windows Defender comes free and is good: keep it on.

Do I need to know about networks?

No. You install GUARDIANA on your PC and, if you want to cover the phones and the TV, you follow a two-minute guide for your router or for each device. The panel explains everything in one sentence.

What happens if I turn the PC off?

Your PC keeps browsing normally, because it keeps its usual DNS as a backup. The devices you pointed at the PC use the backup DNS you left in the router: nothing breaks, they just stop being watched while the PC is off, and when you turn it on GUARDIANA tells you how long it was not looking.

Why is it free?

Because the complete guardian is what makes us grow and it stays that way: open source, no account, no server of ours. What we charge for is the human layer: up-to-date lists explained in plain language, the weekly report, several homes and priority support (Plus, with 7 days free). No ads and no data sales.

How does it work: DNS, a proxy or a driver?

DNS. Almost everything that goes out to the internet starts by asking «where is this service?», and GUARDIANA is what answers that question on your PC (and in your home, with Home Mode). It is not a proxy and not a driver: it does not sit in the middle of the traffic and never opens what you send or receive; it only sees the name that was asked for, the time and which device asked.

What about traffic over a VPN, encrypted DNS or a fixed IP?

It does not see it, and it says so. A VPN, an app with its own encrypted DNS (DoH/DoT), iCloud Private Relay or a program that uses a fixed IP skip the name question. When GUARDIANA detects that a device is trying that path, it writes it down as a signal («DNS evasion») so you know there is traffic it is not watching. The full list of what it does not do is published next to the code.
